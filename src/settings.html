Connecting your key lets every collaborator’s Jev formulas use your TypeSafe account and API usage. Each nonblank formula sends its supplied data, question, and choices or rubric to TypeSafe for processing. Raw JEV(...) sends the supplied JSON. The key authenticates those requests.

After connecting or disconnecting, use Extensions → Jev → Refresh Jev formulas. Existing results may remain visible until refreshed.
